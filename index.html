The Venue

A house for celebration.

White marble floors, arched windows and soft garden greenery — a timeless backdrop that lets your celebration take centre stage.

Casa Poblacion sits right in the heart of Plaridel, easy for family and guests to reach. Our hall dresses up beautifully for every mood, from all-white garden romance to dramatic red-and-gold debut nights.

We believe an elegant event shouldn't be out of reach. Tell us your date and your dream, and we'll help you plan the rest.
